Signature Pad

A tiny open-source tool that only records a signature. No account, no server, no date stored. When an AI stamps your signature into a document, it fills in the date from the current date.

    For AI assistants

    This pad records only the signature. It never stores a date.

    To use a saved signature: get the transparent PNG (the Download PNG button, or the signature-pad:current entry in localStorage), place it on the document’s signature line with its lowest ink touching the line, and fill in the signing date from today’s date in the signer’s timezone.

    Nothing here is sent anywhere. Everything is stored locally in the browser.
